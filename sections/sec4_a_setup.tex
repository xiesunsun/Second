\section{Experimental Studies}
\label{sec:experiments}

This section evaluates SP-PD on 36 public classification datasets, comprising 24 high-dimensional benchmarks and 12 real-world clinical datasets. We first detail the experimental setup, then isolate SP-PD's finite-budget evolutionary search on high-dimensional benchmarks, next verify single-call semantic prior acceleration and misalignment recovery on clinical datasets, and finally conduct ablation, sensitivity, and efficiency studies. All experiments were implemented in Python~3.9, with offline semantic priors queried from Gemini-3.8-Flash.

\subsection{Experimental Setup and Protocols}
\label{sec:exp_setup}

Table~\ref{tab:datasets} summarizes the properties of all 36 public datasets. The 24 high-dimensional benchmarks span gene expression, text, and image recognition with up to 10,000 features and pronounced class imbalance, while the 12 clinical datasets are binary medical tasks with readable feature descriptions. We compare SP-PD against four evolutionary feature selection algorithms, namely GA~\cite{holland1992ga}, BPSO~\cite{kennedy1997bpso}, EDA~\cite{baluja1994pbil}, and MEL~\cite{wang2024mel}, along with four filter and embedded methods, including mRMR~\cite{peng2005mrmr}, ReliefF~\cite{kononenko1994relieff}, L1-LR~\cite{tibshirani1996lasso}, and RF-Imp~\cite{breiman2001rf}. On the clinical datasets, we additionally include $\pi$BO~\cite{hvarfner2022pibo}, LLM-Lasso~\cite{zhang2025llmlasso}, LSP~\cite{skinner2026lsp}, and LLM-Select~\cite{jeong2024llmselect}; LLM-based methods are excluded from high-dimensional benchmarks because their features lack domain semantics and prompting scales linearly with dimensionality~\cite{zhang2025llmlasso,jeong2024llmselect}.

On the high-dimensional benchmarks, all methods use three-fold stratified outer cross-validation with a ten-fold inner 5-NN evaluator and an outer 5-NN classifier under budget $B = 1{,}000$~\cite{wang2024mel,tran2019vlpso,li2023mfcso}. All 24 benchmarks are evaluated under $K = 8$, while 14 high-dimensional benchmarks are additionally tested under $K = \lfloor 0.15p \rfloor$, where $p$ is the number of features. On the clinical datasets, we perform 20 repeated splits at $n \in \{3, 10, 20, 30\}$ training instances per class with $B = 128$ and $K = 4$. Because 5-NN is undefined at $n = 3$, inner evaluation uses a nearest-centroid (NC) classifier under leave-one-pair-out validation. For $n \ge 10$, we additionally evaluate selected subsets using regularized logistic regression (LR) and random forest (RF) classifiers. SP-PD runs the same framework throughout, using data-driven initialization on high-dimensional benchmarks and one offline LLM query ($R = 10$, $\lambda = 0.5$) under aligned and misaligned priors on clinical datasets; algorithm parameters appear in Appendix Table~\ref{tab:app_params}. To account for class imbalance, performance is evaluated by macro-precision, balanced accuracy (BA), macro-F1, and the number of selected features~\cite{xue2016survey}. Statistical significance across datasets is assessed via the Wilcoxon signed-rank test ($\alpha = 0.05$) and Friedman average ranks~\cite{demsar2006stats}.
